**BR3XALITY** (Formerly TotallyNotFreeModel Productions, and Freebee C0de Productions) is a 3-man team, consisting of [Luis Vega](https://br3xawiki.wikioasis.org/wiki/Luis_Vega), [Dylan Hernandez](https://br3xawiki.wikioasis.org/wiki/Dylan_Hernandez), and [Logan Diaz](https://br3xawiki.wikioasis.org/wiki/Logan_Diaz).

## History

Started around July 1st 2024, the team used to be called TotallyNotFreeModel Productions.  

The team was originally a Roblox game making team, until on November 12 2024, [Luis Vega](https://br3xawiki.wikioasis.org/wiki/Luis_Vega) (The CEO of BR3XALITY) decided to name it "BR3XALITY", a combination of Boosted and Reality.

When the team first started, [Luis Vega](https://br3xawiki.wikioasis.org/wiki/Luis_Vega) made Roblox games entirely with free models (hence the name "TotallyNotFreeModel Productions"), these games include The Hole and [BR3XALITY's PNG Game](https://br3xawiki.wikioasis.org/wiki/BR3XALITY's_PNG_Game). But then he decided to get Linux, which did not support Roblox Studio, so then the name was changed to Freebee C0de Productions (which lasted like a week) before he finally changed the name to BR3XALITY.

Once he changed the name, [Dylan Hernandez](https://br3xawiki.wikioasis.org/wiki/Dylan_Hernandez) joined in 2024, and in early November 2025 [Logan Diaz](https://br3xawiki.wikioasis.org/wiki/Logan_Diaz) joined.

They have since made a variety of projects and subsidiaries.
